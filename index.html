Millions of footsteps move through India’s malls, airports, offices, and public spaces every day. That kinetic energy is currently wasted. Existing energy-generating flooring solutions often prioritize functionality over aesthetics, limiting adoption in premium architectural spaces.

KINERA fuses precision piezoelectric engineering with designer-grade tile craftsmanship. Every tile contributes to measurable energy generation while elevating premium interior and exterior spaces.
